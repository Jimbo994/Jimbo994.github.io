I am a **Data Scientist at [Evonik](https://www.evonik.com/)**, working on machine learning for chemistry and analytical workflows. My interests include Bayesian optimization, experimental automation, liquid chromatography method development, deep learning, and molecular modeling.

I completed my PhD in machine learning and chemistry at the [University of Amsterdam](https://www.uva.nl/) in 2024. My research brought together the [Amsterdam Machine Learning Lab](https://amlab.science.uva.nl/), [AI4Science](https://ai4science-amsterdam.github.io/), and the [Chemometrics and Advanced Separations Team](https://cast-amsterdam.org/), under the supervision of Bernd Ensing, Bob Pirok, and Patrick Forré.
